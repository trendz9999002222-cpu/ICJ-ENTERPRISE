## 2. Dynamic Role Governance Matrix

- **Client:** Registrations, Filing Petitions, Online Payments, Order Downloads, Invoice Downloads, Live Case Status Tracking.
- **Empaneled Advocate:** Assigned Case Queue, AI Drafter, Court Hearing Calendar, Confidential Notes, Earnings Statements (70% Share).
- **Trust Executive Board:** Petition Approvals/Rejections, Advocate Empanelment, Revenue Share Audit, Governance Monitoring.
- **Administrator:** Master System Settings, Role & Access Control, Offline Cash Receipts, Refund Approvals, System Data Backup.

---

## 3. Financial & Billing Governance

- **18% GST:** Mandatory on all legal service invoices.
- **Revenue Split Engine:** 70% Advocate Share / 30% ICJ Trust Revenue Share.
- **TDS Deduction:** 10% under Section 194J on advocate payouts.
- **Prevent Duplicate Payments:** 10-second threshold check on payment transactions.
